DisableHover v9 - separate toolbar buttons

DH = toggles Disable Blue Highlights only.
DT = toggles Disable Tooltips only.

The DH button does NOT disable/intercept the game's raycast or selection input.
It only suppresses the native blue hover/highlight rendering, avoiding the old
Harmony side effects with train-line tools and double-click building selection.

Expected logs:
Toolbar DH clicked. Disable Blue Highlights = True/False
Toolbar DT clicked. Disable Tooltips = True/False
